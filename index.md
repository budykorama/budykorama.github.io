# Budykorama

*Budyorama* is a community of scientists with the shared goal of advancing mechanistic understanding of land-climate science. Inspired by Mikhail Budyko, who developed foundational concepts in [terrestrial hydrology](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2020.103451) and [global climate dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0469(1975)032<2033:TOEBCM>2.0.CO;2), the Budykorama community has organised annual workshops since 2022 and written a [perspective article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-024-01553-8) on the future of land-climate science.
