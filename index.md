# Budykorama

*Budyorama* is a community of scientists with the shared goal of advancing mechanistic understanding of land climate. Inspired by Mikhail Budyko, who developed foundational concepts in [hydrology](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2020.103451) and [global climate dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0469(1975)032<2033:TOEBCM>2.0.CO;2), the Budykorama community has organised annual [workshops](https://budykorama.github.io/workshops/) since 2022 and written a [perspective](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-024-01553-8) on the future of the field. [We](https://budykorama.github.io/people/) aim to develop Budyorama over coming years, with activities focused on key questions at the forefront of land-climate science.
